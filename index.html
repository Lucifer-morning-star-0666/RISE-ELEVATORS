About Rise Elevators

Lift systems designed around safety, reliability, and long-term support.

Rise Elevators helps property owners, developers, and hospitals bring dependable vertical mobility to their spaces. From first consultation and installation to maintenance, AMC, and modernisation, every project is handled with compliance, clarity, and service accountability.

Based in Malikpur, Pathankot, the company serves clients across Punjab with practical system guidance, responsive service, and product recommendations tailored to building type and traffic needs.
