1. Project Overview
Rise Elevators is a Pathankot-based elevator company offering installation, maintenance, AMC, and
modernisation services across Punjab. The goal is to build a modern, mobile-friendly, single-brand website
using pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks — that showcases their products, builds trust, and
generates leads.

5. Section-by-Section Requirements
5.1 Sticky Navigation Bar
 Logo (Rise Elevators SVG/PNG) on the left.
 Nav links: Home · About · Products · Services · Why Us · Gallery · Contact.
 Hamburger menu on mobile (CSS-only toggle with JS fallback).
 Active link highlighted in orange on scroll (IntersectionObserver).
 'Get a Quote' CTA button — orange pill, opens contact section.
 Background turns navy + box-shadow after scrolling 80 px.
5.2 Hero Section
 Full-viewport-height background — dark overlay on elevator image.
 Headline: 'Where Innovation Meets Elevation' (from brand document).
 Sub-headline: 'Safe · Reliable · Affordable Elevators in Punjab'.
 Two CTAs: 'Explore Products' (navy) and 'WhatsApp Us' (green #25D366).
 Animated counter strip below hero: Years of Experience · Projects Completed · Cities Served · Happy
Clients.
5.3 About Section
 Two-column layout: text left, founder photo + brand card right.
 Company overview from brochure — mission-driven copy.
 Founder card: Charanjit Bhatti, Founder & Director.
 Small trust badges: 'Indian Standards Certified', 'Factory Act Compliant'.
5.4 Products Section
 Section title + orange underline accent.
 6 product cards in a responsive CSS Grid (3-col → 2-col → 1-col).
 Each card: icon/image, product name, short description, 'Learn More' button.
 Products: Home Lift · Goods & Freight Elevator · Hospital/Stretcher Elevator · Hydraulic Elevator ·
Passenger Elevator · Glass/Panoramic Elevator.
 'Learn More' triggers a modal/drawer with full description from brochure.
5.5 Services Section
 Icon + title + one-line description for each service.
 4 services in a 2×2 or 4-col row: Lift Installation · Maintenance & Repair · AMC (Annual Maintenance
Contract) · Lift Modernisation.
 Each service card has a hover lift shadow and orange icon.
5.6 Why Choose Us
 3–4 feature blocks: Noiseless & Easy Install · Small Space Required · Safety Certified · 24/7 Support.
 Animated number counters (triggered when section enters viewport): 500+ Projects · 10+ Years · 50+
Cities · 100% Safety Record.
5.7 Gallery Section
 Masonry or uniform grid of 8–12 project images.

 Lightbox on click (vanilla JS — no library required).
 Filter tabs: All · Home Lifts · Commercial · Hospital · Industrial.
5.8 Testimonials
 Auto-sliding carousel (CSS transitions + JS interval).
 Pause on hover. Prev/Next arrows. Dot indicators.
 Each card: quote, client name, city, star rating.
5.9 Contact Section
 Left column: contact form (Name · Phone · Email · Product Interest dropdown · Message · Submit).
 Right column: address card, phone numbers, email, Google Maps embed (iframe).
 Floating WhatsApp button (bottom-right) — always visible on all pages.
 Form validation in JS — no empty required fields; phone must be 10 digits.
 On successful submit: success toast notification (no backend needed — mailto or Formspree).
5.10 Footer
 Dark navy background.
 3 columns: Brand info · Quick Links · Contact Details.
 Social icons: WhatsApp · Facebook · Instagram.
 Bottom strip: © 2025 Rise Elevators. All Rights Reserved.

6.3 Spacing & Layout
 Base unit: 8 px. All spacing multiples of 8 (8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 80, 96).
 Max content width: 1200 px, centred with auto margins.
 Section padding: 80px top/bottom desktop · 48px mobile.
 Border-radius: buttons 6px · cards 12px · modals 16px.
6.4 Animation Guidelines
 Use CSS transitions for hover/focus — duration 250–350 ms, ease-out.
 Scroll animations: 'fade-up' via IntersectionObserver — stagger 100 ms per card.
 Counter animation: requestAnimationFrame, 1.5 s duration.
 Carousel: CSS translateX + JS class toggle — no jQuery.
 Respect prefers-reduced-motion — disable non-essential animations.

10. SEO & Performance
Meta Tags (in )
<title>Rise Elevators | Safe & Reliable Elevators in Pathankot, Punjab</title>
<meta name="description" content="Rise Elevators — Home lifts, Goods lifts, Hospital
lifts, Hydraulic lifts. Installation, AMC & Modernisation in Punjab. Call +91 92172
53635">
<meta name="keywords" content="elevator company Pathankot, lift installation Punjab,
home lift, hospital elevator, AMC lift service">
<meta property="og:title" content="Rise Elevators — Where Innovation Meets Elevation">
<link rel="canonical" href="https://www.riseelevators.in/">
Local SEO
 Include full NAP (Name, Address, Phone) in footer — consistent with Google Business Profile.
 Embed Google Maps with 'Rise Elevators' marker.
 Add LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema in .
 Use location keywords naturally: Pathankot, Punjab, Malikpur in H1/H2.
Performance Checklist
 Use <img loading='lazy'> for all below-fold images.
 Serve images in WebP, fallback to JPEG.
 Minify CSS and JS before deployment (use Terser / CSSNano).
 Use Google Fonts with display=swap and preconnect.
 Avoid render-blocking scripts — all JS at end of body or defer attribute.
